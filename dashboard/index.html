How to use the dashboard

1Pick a period

Click a Year tile at the top (click a second year for a range) and any Month tiles, or use Quick period on the Overview page.

2Narrow by month

Open Months to keep only some months, for example Q2 (April–June) across every year in your period.

3Choose report types

Open Report types in the slicer bar, or use the buttons on the Report types page. Each shows how many reports match your other choices. Tip: click a card in What each report type covers below to jump straight to that type.

4Choose regions and states

Use Region and State in the Filters panel on the left (all 36 states and the FCT, with counts), or click a state on the Map. Click a state to focus on it, click more to add them, click again to remove.

5Read the headline numbers

The cards on the Overview page give the total, the monthly average, the busiest month and the latest report, and compare with the previous period of the same length.

6Explore the charts

Hover over any bar, point or cell to see its number. Click a bar in report type, state or region to filter by it; click a calendar or table cell to list those reports.

7Open or export reports

The Report list page links to each report on dtm.iom.int and its PDF. Export CSV and Download counts save what you are looking at.

8Ask the data

Use the Ask the data button (bottom right) to type questions such as “flash reports per month in Borno in 2025”.

What each report type covers

Good to know

How reports are dated

Each report counts in the month it was published on dtm.iom.int. You can switch to the period named in each report title. Reports published twice count once; a report with no date on the site is flagged in the list.

Mobility Tracking: one report per round

Each round counts as exactly one round report: 71 so far (North East R1–R52, North Central & North West R1–R19). North-East rounds 1–40 and NC/NW rounds 1–9 are Needs Monitoring; later rounds are classified from the PDF's file name or the text inside it. All other round files count as Mobility Tracking – Dashboards.

Regions and states

A report counts for a state only when it was published for that state: the state is named in its title (for ETT reports, on the report itself). States merely mentioned inside a report do not count, and the Republic of Niger is never counted as Niger State. DTM Nigeria runs the Emergency Tracking Tool in Adamawa, Borno, Yobe and Benue; ETT reports from before July 2024 that do not name a state count for the North East.

Always up to date

New reports on dtm.iom.int are collected every hour, with a full check every night. An open dashboard refreshes itself within 5 minutes; the offline copy works without internet.

Map – numbers by state

Reports published shades each state by the number of DTM reports published for it on dtm.iom.int, for the period and report types you selected. Mobility Tracking figures come from the latest rounds you choose (North East states from the North East round, all other states from the North Central & North West round). Incident / flash figures add up what each flash report states, for the period, region and states selected in the slicer bar. Hover or click a state to see all its numbers.
