How to use the dashboard

1Pick a period

In the blue panel on the left, set From and To to any month and year, or use a shortcut such as Last 12 months or Last year.

2Narrow by month

Open Months to keep only some months, for example Q2 (April–June) across every year in your period.

3Choose report types

Use the bold Report types buttons at the top or the dropdown on the left. Each shows how many reports match your other choices. Tip: click a card in What each report type covers below to jump straight to that type.

4Choose regions and states

Use the Region and States dropdowns on the left (all 36 states and the FCT, with counts) or the bold buttons at the top. Click a state to focus on it, click more to add them, click again to remove.

5Read the headline numbers

The cards at the top give the total, the monthly average, the busiest month and the latest report, and compare with the previous period of the same length.

6Explore the charts

Hover over any bar, point or cell to see its number. Click a bar in report type, state or region to filter by it; click a calendar or table cell to list those reports.

7Open or export reports

The report list at the bottom links to each report on dtm.iom.int and its PDF. Export CSV and Download counts save what you are looking at.

8Ask the data

Use the Ask the data button (bottom right) to type questions such as “flash reports per month in Borno in 2025”.

What each report type covers

Good to know

How reports are dated

Each report counts in the month it was published on dtm.iom.int. You can switch to the period named in each report title. Reports published twice count once; a report with no date on the site is flagged in the list.

Needs Monitoring and Atlas

North-East rounds 1–40 and North-Central/North-West rounds 1–9 count as Needs Monitoring. Later rounds are classified from the report's PDF file name or, if needed, the text inside the PDF.

Regions and states

A state named in a report's title decides its region. Mobility Tracking round reports belong to the operation that produced them: North East, or North Central & North West.

Always up to date

New reports on dtm.iom.int are collected every hour, with a full check every night. An open dashboard refreshes itself within 5 minutes; the offline copy works without internet.
